Questions

Procurement FAQs.

Common questions from procurement and trading teams.

What's the minimum order quantity?

ICUMSA 45 sugar typically starts at 12,500 MT. Frozen poultry starts at 4–6 containers. Energy contracts are structured per product and destination — EN590 usually starts at 25,000 MT. Soy and smaller-volume products can be quoted per contract.

Which certifications do you provide?

Every shipment carries GACC (for China), ISO 22000 (food safety), HACCP (hazard control), and Halal certificates as required — plus origin certificates, phytosanitary certificates, and lot-level traceability documents.

What are typical lead times?

From confirmed contract to delivery, 30–60 days depending on origin, destination, and shipping route. Cold-chain and time-sensitive cargo may be expedited to 21–30 days.

How do you handle quality assurance?

Every lot is inspected at origin by our QC partners before loading. We provide inspection reports (SGS or equivalent), and offer third-party inspection at destination on request.

Can you handle end-to-end logistics (DDP)?

Yes — DDP is one of our core delivery structures. We handle ocean freight, port handling, customs clearance, duties, and final-mile delivery to your warehouse.
