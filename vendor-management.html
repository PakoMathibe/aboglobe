Twelve Workflows

Every step, automated.

From vendor self-registration through business intelligence — twelve workflows that cover the entire procurement lifecycle.

01

Vendor self-registration

Define what information you need from vendors to work productively forever after — with relevant steps, fill-in fields, and document requirements built into the registration process.

02

Vendor onboarding

Set a vendor assessment framework so the VMS automatically scores registered vendors against your criteria. This pre-onboarding step builds a low-risk vendor portfolio from day one.

03

Vendor profile management

Vendors manage their own business information independently. This keeps profiles up to date automatically — no direct control required from your team.

04

Vendor segmentation

Plan and implement the best-suited segmentation model for your supplier base — for example, give the highest strategic priority to suppliers of high-volume or high-value products.

05

Purchase requisition

Create as many requisition templates as needed — one per department, one per procurement purpose, one per commodity category. Each describes the required products or services.

06

Requisition approval

The VMS routes requisitions through multi-level approval workflows and sends automated notifications to responsible employees according to the rules you set. Approvals can span departments.

07

Request for Quote (RFQ)

Suppliers are notified when invited to participate in a bidding process. They can submit their offering for direct price competition — no manual coordination required.

08

Processing bid responses

When the bid submission period expires, evaluate bids side by side and decide on a vendor of choice. No manual tracking of emails, no risk of missing a price quote.

09

Automated PO creation

If price bidding isn't needed, set the VMS to create purchase orders automatically from approved requisitions and send them to the chosen vendor — one fewer manual step, no procurement delays.

10

Invoicing

Invoices are created automatically from an approved purchase order (or any part of one). Taxes are applied based on origin- and destination-based rules. The invoice is immediately ready to pay.

11

Order management

Full visibility into every procurement order. Track progress, contact vendors for delay explanations, and access shipment documents — all in one place.

12

Business intelligence

Built-in analytics turn vendor performance data into actionable insight — uncovering opportunities for cost savings and risk mitigation across the entire supplier base.

Integrations

Fits into your existing stack.

The VMS connects to the tools your team already uses — accounting, ERP, HR, and intranet.

VMS + Accounting software

  • Deliver invoices directly to the responsible team — eliminating protractions caused by manual transfers.
  • Pay contractors from the same system where you book and track their work.

VMS + ERP

  • Rely on manufacturing capacity plans for raw material procurement — avoiding understocks and overstocks.
  • Provide visibility into procurement order statuses for staff without a VMS login, such as the procurement planning team.

VMS + HR management

  • Surface current talent gaps and vacant positions needed for a limited period — for example, temporary specialists for a project.

VMS + Intranet

  • Collect office supply requests directly from staff through the intranet, then organize them into requisition lists automatically.
Success Factors

What makes it work.

Four foundational capabilities that turn the VMS from a tool into a competitive advantage.

E-document management

Create, e-sign, and exchange documents with vendors digitally — from contracts and certificates to POs and invoices. No paper. No printing. No lost emails.

Vendor rating functionality

Automatically score vendors based on user-defined performance assessment criteria. Turn subjective "they feel reliable" into a measurable, comparable, audit-ready number.

Maximized workflow automation

Eliminate manual effort across every rule-based vendor management task — approvals, payment initiation and control, escalation of delays and challenges, and everything in between.

Well-designed checklists

Onboarding and offboarding checklists that cover documentation, work instructions, access to corporate resources, and team introductions — for every role, every subsidiary, and remote or in-office positions.
