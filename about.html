Our Story

From local roots to global reach.

Three chapters that define who we are — a Vancouver-born trading house that grew into a three-continent operation across commodities, finance, and real estate.

Vancouver roots, Pacific Rim vision.

ABOGLOBE International INC (1463323 BC LTD) began as a BC-registered trading company with a sharp focus on the Pacific Rim. What started as a lean export operation quickly grew into a trusted name across Canadian and Asian trade corridors.

As a wholly-owned subsidiary of ZM PROPERTY TRUST GROUP LTD, we established the compliance-first foundation that still governs every shipment and every contract we sign.

BC Reg. #1463323 Subsidiary of ZM Property Trust

Three continents, one supply chain.

Direct relationships with manufacturers and distributors across Brazil, Malaysia, China, and Thailand opened the door to large-scale commodity flows — white sugar, premium poultry, soybean products, edible oils, beef, and energy resources (EN590, D6, LNG).

We complete investment and sales of bulk trade commodities through OTG, DDP, FOB, and CIF — with every shipment arriving fully certified, inspected, and traceable from source to destination.

Brazil · Malaysia · China OTG · DDP · FOB · CIF

Finance and certified medical — same standards.

Our dedicated financial investment and financing division focuses on DLC, SBLC, and BG instruments, plus private equity, private credit, real assets, and hedge fund partnerships — backed by established international banking relationships.

In parallel, our Medical & PPE arm distributes 3M, Cranberry, and Cardinal Health products from certified facilities across Thailand, China, and Vietnam — fully FDA 510K, CE, and ISO certified.
