Instruments

Diverse financial instruments.

Secure, compliant instruments to facilitate international business.

DLC

Documentary Letters of Credit

Ensuring safe and transparent transactions between buyers and sellers across borders — the standard instrument for import/export finance.

Ideal for: Trade finance · import/export · one-time transactions
  • Tier-1 issuing banks
  • Fast issuance (7–14 days)

SBLC

Standby Letters of Credit

Providing strong guarantees for high-value trade and investment operations — a bank-backed commitment that underwrites performance.

Ideal for: High-value transactions · guarantees · multi-year contracts
  • Multi-year terms available
  • Wide international acceptance

BG

Bank Guarantees

Supporting complex agreements and building trust in global financial transactions — performance, advance payment, and retention guarantees.

Ideal for: Complex agreements · performance · EPC contracts
  • Performance & payment
  • SWIFT-authenticated

Questions

Investor FAQs.

Common questions from trade finance and structured investment clients.

What's the minimum size for a DLC or SBLC?

Typical minimum is $5M USD. Below that level, banks generally don't find the fee economics workable. For strategic clients and long-term relationships, we can structure smaller tranches under a master facility.

How long does issuance take?

From complete KYC pack: 7–14 business days for standard DLCs, 10–21 days for SBLCs and BGs. Expedited issuance is available for established clients.

Which banks do you work with?

We work with 18 tier-1 banks across Europe, Asia, and North America. Specific bank selection depends on your jurisdiction, currency, and deal structure — we recommend the best fit.

What KYC documentation is required?

Standard corporate pack: certificate of incorporation, beneficial ownership chart, director IDs, last 2 years of audited financials, and expected transaction profile. Enhanced due diligence may be required for high-risk jurisdictions.

Can you structure instruments for commodity deals specifically?

Yes — our trade finance desk specializes in commodity-linked instruments. We're experienced with letters of credit for sugar, poultry, soy, and energy products, including GACC-certified and FDA-compliant flows.
