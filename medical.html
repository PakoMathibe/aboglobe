Questions

Procurement FAQs.

Everything your procurement and compliance teams need to know.

Are your products fully certified?

Yes — every product we distribute carries FDA 510K (US), CE (EU), and ISO 13485 certification. Each shipment includes a complete certificate pack, batch traceability documentation, and origin certificates.

What are your minimum order quantities?

PPE can be shipped as low as a single pallet for emergency needs. Standard re-orders start at 4–6 pallets. Bulk government programs can be structured for 10,000+ units per product.

How long does delivery take?

For in-stock products, delivery is typically 14–30 days depending on destination and trade terms. Emergency shipments can be expedited to 5–7 days for verified healthcare and government clients.

Do you support government and hospital procurement processes?

Yes — we're experienced with public tenders, RFP processes, and hospital procurement portals. We supply audit-ready documentation, compliance certificates, and delivery confirmations.

Which trade terms do you support?

We work under OTG, DDP, FOB, and CIF. Payment terms are typically net-30 for qualified institutional clients, with DLC and SBLC options for larger volume programs.
