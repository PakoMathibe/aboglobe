Questions

Common questions.

What's the fastest way to reach your team?

Call us at 778-744-3806, email drli@aboglobe.com, or use the form above — we respond within 24 business hours. For urgent shipments, ask for the on-call duty officer.

Do you handle international shipping?

Yes — we support OTG, DDP, FOB, and CIF terms across all our commodity, medical, and PPE product lines. DDP means we handle delivery to your warehouse door.

Can I request samples?

Yes — sample shipments are available for qualified buyers on commodity, medical, and PPE product lines. Contact our trading desk for details and sample terms.

Where can I find product documentation?

All shipments include full certificate packs (GACC, ISO 22000, HACCP, Halal, FDA 510K, CE, ISO 13485 as applicable). For pre-purchase documentation, contact our team directly.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes — mutual NDAs are standard for detailed commodity, investment, and pricing discussions. Tick the NDA checkbox on the form or ask your advisor.
