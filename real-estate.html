Questions

Investor FAQs.

Common questions from buyers, owners, and international investors.

Can international buyers purchase ABOGLOBE properties?

Yes — international buyers are welcome. BC's foreign buyer tax applies to certain properties, and our team will walk you through the additional 20% tax, exemptions, and structuring options before you commit.

What deposit structure do you offer on pre-sale?

Typically 5% on contract, 5% at six months, and 5% at completion. For larger commitments, we can structure the deposit across milestones to align with your capital deployment timeline.

Do you offer financing?

We work with several Canadian banks and private lenders who specialize in pre-sale and construction financing for foreign and domestic buyers. We can introduce you to vetted partners.

How do you ensure build quality?

Every project is built by vetted general contractors and inspected by independent third-party consultants at framing, envelope, MEP, and final stages. Materials are sourced from certified suppliers.

What happens after handover?

All projects come with the standard BC 2-5-10 warranty, plus our own 12-month post-completion service desk for warranty items and minor fixes.
