Questions

Frequently asked.

Everything you need to know before engaging with our team.

What are your minimum order quantities?

For most commodity transactions we work in container or bulk quantities. ICUMSA 45 sugar typically starts at 12,500 MT, frozen chicken at 4–6 containers, and PPE can be shipped as low as a single pallet for emergency needs.

Which trade terms do you support?

We work under OTG, DDP, FOB, and CIF. For financial instruments, we support DLC, SBLC, and BG through established international banking partners.

Are your PPE products fully certified?

Yes — all 3M, Cranberry, and Cardinal Health products we distribute carry FDA 510K, CE, and ISO certifications with full certificate packs.

How long does a typical transaction take?

From inquiry to delivery, most commodity transactions close within 30–60 days. Financial instruments can be structured in as little as 7–14 business days.

Can I visit your Vancouver headquarters?

Absolutely. Our Richmond, BC office welcomes partners by appointment. Reach out via our contact page.
